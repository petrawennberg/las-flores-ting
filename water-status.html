Water Status

Current conservation level, water sources, and infrastructure updates.

Report Issue: 626-797-1138 Pay Online

Conservation Level

CRITICAL: Critical water conservation status in effect. Service suspended for fire-damaged properties effective January 8, 2025.

Las Flores Water Co. is currently at CRITICAL (Red Alert) status due to the Eaton Fire. Outdoor irrigation is strictly limited to 2 days per week maximum.

Water Sources

Mount View Well
~35% of supply
Foothill MWD (Metropolitan)
~65% of supply

Water is treated, maintained, and distributed to central and northern Altadena, east of Mountain View Cemetery.

Current Restrictions

  • Outdoor irrigation: 2 days per week maximum
  • No irrigation within 48 hours of measurable rainfall
  • No hosing driveways or sidewalks
  • Hoses must have a shutoff nozzle
  • Decorative water features must use recirculating systems
  • Report leaks immediately: 626-797-1138
  • Visit bewaterwise.com for conservation tips
