Shareholder & Ownership

Las Flores is a mutual water company. Each property in the service area holds shares. Ownership transfers with the property.

No. Shares are appurtenant to the land and transfer with the property.

Check the "Stock Credit" line on your bill. Each dollar listed equals one share.

Certificates are kept on file at the Las Flores office.

Each share is entitled to one vote at the Annual Shareholder Meeting. Proxy voting is available. Bring proxy documentation if attending on behalf of another shareholder.

The regular annual meeting is the third Monday of March at 7:00 PM. The 2026 meeting is March 16. Open to the public.
