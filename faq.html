Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about billing, water service, and shareholder ownership.

Billing

Bills are due upon receipt. Late fees are assessed automatically when unpaid balances reach $25 or more.

Visit lasfloresviewmybill.net or call the office at 626-797-1138 during business hours.

You can pay online at lasfloresviewmybill.net, by phone through SOFTtelPay (855-483-5729), by mail to PO Box 426 Altadena CA 91003-0426, by direct debit, or in person on Mondays at 428 E Sacramento St.

SOFTtelPay charges $1.95–$7.95 depending on payment amount, or 4% for amounts over $210. The direct online portal at lasfloresviewmybill.net has no convenience fee.

Service

Shareholder & Ownership

Board meetings are typically held the third Monday of each month at 7:00 PM, except August. See the Governance page for the full 2026 schedule.

The annual shareholder meeting is held the third Monday of March at 7:00 PM. It is open to all shareholders and the public.

Home sales require a stock transfer ($75 fee) processed through escrow. Contact the office to initiate the transfer.

Tenant occupancy requires closing the owner's service account and opening one in the tenant's name. The property owner remains liable for any unpaid bills.

Over 1,400 shareholders constitute the ownership of Las Flores Water Company.

Yes, board meetings are open to shareholders and the public.

The board consists of 5 elected directors chosen by shareholders. A 20% quorum is required for shareholder meetings where elections take place.
