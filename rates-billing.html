Rates & Billing

Water rates, payment options, and financial assistance.

Water Rates

Rates effective April 1, 2025

Tier Usage (HCF/month) Rate per HCF
Tier 1 0–8 HCF $5.26
Tier 2 9–20 HCF $6.15
Tier 3 21–35 HCF $7.12
Tier 4 36+ HCF $8.17

Monthly service charge: $39.45

1 HCF = 748 gallons. The service charge covers fixed expenses including infrastructure maintenance, employee salaries, office expenses, utilities, and insurance.

Billing Policies

  • Late fees assessed automatically when unpaid balance reaches $25+
  • One courtesy reversal per 12-month period
  • Service charge of $30.32/month applies even when water is shut off
  • Bank bill-pay arrives as mailed check (may cause delays)
