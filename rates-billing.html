Water Rates

Rates effective April 1, 2025

Tier Usage (HCF/month) Rate per HCF
Tier 1 1–10 HCF $5.26
Tier 2 10.1–20 HCF $5.76
Tier 3 20.1–39 HCF $6.76
Tier 4 39.1+ HCF $8.17

Monthly service charge: $39.45 per connection

Foothill MWD surcharge: $0.07635 per HCF

1 HCF = 748 gallons. The service charge covers fixed expenses including infrastructure maintenance, employee salaries, office expenses, utilities, and insurance.

Financial Assistance

Several state programs offer assistance. Contact the office for current program information.

  • Low Income Household Water Assistance Program (LIHWAP)
  • California Arrearage Payment Program
  • Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)
  • COVID-19 Rent Relief

Billing Policies

  • A $10 late fee is assessed when unpaid balance reaches $25+ at the start of the next billing cycle
  • One courtesy reversal per 12-month period
  • Service charge of $30.32/month applies even when water is shut off
  • Bank bill-pay arrives as mailed check (may cause delays)
