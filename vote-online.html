How to Vote Online

Step-by-step guide to casting your shareholder vote through ElectionBuddy.

Before You Start

You will need the following to vote online:

Account Number
Found on your water bill

Look for “Account #” in the top-right corner of your monthly statement.

Email Address
For vote confirmation

ElectionBuddy will send a receipt to confirm your vote was recorded.

Don’t know your account number? Contact the office at 626-797-1138 or email info@lasfloreswaterco.com.

How It Works

1

Go to ElectionBuddy

Visit the Las Flores Water Co. voting page on ElectionBuddy. You can access it directly from the button below or from any “Vote Online” link on this website.

Open ElectionBuddy

2

Verify Your Identity

Enter your shareholder account number when prompted. This confirms you are an eligible voter and determines how many votes (shares) you hold.

3

Review the Proposal

Read the proposal summary on-screen. You can also review the full proposal details on our website before voting.

4

Cast Your Vote

Select For or Against the $3,000 Fire Recovery Fee Proposal. Each share you hold counts as one vote.

5

Confirm & Submit

Review your selection and submit. You will receive an email confirmation that your vote has been recorded. Your vote is anonymous — only the totals are reported.

About ElectionBuddy

ElectionBuddy is a third-party online voting platform used by cooperatives, HOAs, nonprofits, and mutual companies to run secure elections and shareholder votes.

  • Votes are encrypted and anonymous
  • Each account number can only vote once
  • Results are independently verifiable
  • No account registration or app download required

Troubleshooting

My account number isn’t working

Make sure you’re entering the number exactly as it appears on your bill, including any leading zeros. If it still doesn’t work, contact the office to verify your number.

It says I already voted

Each account number can only be used once. If you believe this is an error, contact the office. Your previous vote cannot be changed once submitted.

I didn’t receive a confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from ElectionBuddy. Your vote is still counted even if the confirmation is delayed.

I’d rather vote in person or by mail

No problem. See the full proposal page for instructions on voting in person at the March 16 meeting or submitting a proxy card by mail.
